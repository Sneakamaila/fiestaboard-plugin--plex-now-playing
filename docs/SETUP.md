# Plex Now Playing - Setup Guide

## 1. Get Your Plex Token
1. Open Plex in your browser, play media, and click 'Get Info' -> 'View XML'.
2. Grab the 'X-Plex-Token=XXXX' from the URL.

## 2. Config in FiestaBoard
1. Open FiestaBoard -> Integrations -> Enable Plex Now Playing.
2. Enter your URL and token.
